Terms, Policies & Authorization

1. Cancellation & Deposit

48+ hours notice: the trip-expense deposit is refunded or credited toward a future service. Less than 48 hours notice: the deposit is retained to cover fuel and supply costs already committed to the appointment.

2. Rescheduling

24+ hours notice: the deposit transfers 100% to the new date or to a different service of equal or greater value. Less than 24 hours notice or a no-show: the deposit is forfeited and a new deposit is required to rebook.

3. Access & Utilities

Client agrees to provide safe property access, running hot/cold water, and functional electricity during scheduled service hours.

4. Inclement Weather

For exterior services or when travel is unsafe, Exhale Elite Maids will proactively reschedule at no penalty. The deposit carries over to the next available date.

5. Scope & Changes

Final scope and pricing are confirmed in writing before booking. Material changes in property condition, access, square footage, or requested work may require a written change to the proposal price.
